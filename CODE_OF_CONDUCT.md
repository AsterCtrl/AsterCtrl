# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, safe and inclusive community. Contributors must
be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus discussion on technical work,
and avoid harassment, discrimination or disclosure of private information.

Project maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently exclude participants whose conduct harms the community. Report
problems privately to the security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews, chat, events and
other spaces where somebody represents AsterCtrl.
